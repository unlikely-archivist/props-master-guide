## Blocked / open

1. **The Prop Effects Guidebook file is truncated.** The uploaded EPUB (`…Prop_Effects_Guidebook…epub`, 4,941,770 bytes) ends partway through; only images, the table of contents, and metadata survived — none of the chapter text. A complete copy (re-upload, or the 2025 2nd edition) is needed to finish [ch. 26](guide/26-effects-props.md). The chapter has a section map showing where each of its 11 chapters will go.
2. **Edition mismatch.** Hart page citations refer to the 2013 first edition. If you adopt the 3rd edition (2024) for class or the textbook, citations will need remapping (topics should map cleanly by chapter title). James citations refer to the 1992 first edition.
3. **Appendix E dates not individually verified.** Most rows repeat James's dates. Rows marked † flag known disagreements, but unmarked rows haven't been checked against other sources. Verify any date before using it in film or close-up work, or before publishing the textbook.
4. **Part of the scanned James text was garbled** (the toy timeline's dates were misaligned in the PDF text layer), so Appendix E uses commonly cited dates for toys.

5. **The Fake Food Cookbook PDF is an OCR scan.** A few quantities were unreadable: popcorn piece size (FF 51–52), doughnut-pan fill depth (FF 73), and the wonton dough's salt and water amounts (FF 65). Ch. 28 and Appendix F describe these instead of giving numbers. Two salt-dough ingredient boxes were read from the page image (FF 21). Most FF citations are at chapter or recipe level (page ranges), not individual lines. FF's product names and prices (2018) are unverified.

6. **KF product names and convention rules are unverified.** KF (2019) names specific primers, fillers, paints and a rotary-tool price, and describes convention weapon rules in general terms. None of these were checked against current sources. KF's text is clean, but many steps point to numbered photos and YouTube videos; ch. 29 describes the steps in words only. One figure is reproduced as printed and may be a typo: magnets "7 mm radius and 2 mm high" (KF 18).
