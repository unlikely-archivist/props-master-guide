# Project Status and Next Steps

*This file is working/handoff context and will go stale. Stable information about the guide itself lives in the [README](README.md) and [Appendix C](appendices/C-sources-and-editions.md).*

**Last updated:** 2026-11-22

## Goal

A **master reference** that stands in for reading every source: readable front to back, and organized so you can look up any part of the job (admin, sourcing, any material, weapons, effects) and find the reference information, methods, safety and projects in one place. See CLAUDE.md for how new sources are integrated toward that goal.

## Ready for the next source
Open a new chat and give the book. CLAUDE.md Step 3 now spells out the fold-in procedure: a topic map first, then agree → add a citation, adds → merge into the existing section, disagrees → neutral inline note plus a table row, fills a gap → replace [General knowledge], tutorials → the material's projects file, and new prop types → Appendix F. All `claude/*` branches are merged into the default branch.

## Blocked / open

0. **Wilson (W) follow-ups.** (a) The PDF had no text layer; it was OCR'd with Tesseract. One drill speed printed as "SOOrpm" was read as 800 rpm from Wilson's own 30 m/min rule; a few photo captions were partly garbled but not needed. (b) W's British regulations, standards and product names (BS 476, BS 5852, BS 679, COSHH, Foamcoat, Idenden, Firecheck, Celastic) are dated; only the BS 476 → BS EN 13501-1 change and the Bosch foam cutter were verified. (c) Disagreements recorded: band saw vs. table saw as the key machine (ch. 29); fire-watch time, galvanized metal lath and vertical weld direction (chs. 20, 30); alginate mold life and urethane in alginate (ch. 38); polyester GRP preference and grease as a release (ch. 36); PCL softening temperature (chs. 31, 37); handling imitation leaf (ch. 28). (d) W's UK supplier list was not transcribed.

1. **General-knowledge chapters need sources.** Chs. 3, 5, 42–45, 51–53 and parts of 9, 15–19, 32, 35 and 46–50 aren't traceable to a source (W now covers parts of 5, 9, 19, 32, 46 and 49; the remaining marked lines are still general knowledge). Suggested books to fill them: Hart's *Prop Effects Guidebook* (effects, chs. 46–50); a stage-combat and theatrical-weapons text (chs. 42–45); a film set-decoration or props text (chs. 3, 51); a props history (ch. 5); a careers guide such as Lawler, *Careers in Technical Theater* (ch. 53). Verify general-knowledge facts (dates, names, laws) before publishing.
2. **The Prop Effects Guidebook file is truncated.** The uploaded EPUB (`…Prop_Effects_Guidebook…epub`, 4,941,770 bytes) ends partway through; only images, the table of contents and metadata survived. A complete copy (re-upload, or the 2025 2nd edition) is needed for chs. 46–50. [Ch. 46](guide/7-effects/46-effects-and-breakaways.md) has a section map showing where each of its 11 chapters will go.
3. **Edition mismatch.** Hart page citations refer to the 2013 first edition. If you adopt the 3rd edition (2024), citations will need remapping. James citations refer to the 1992 first edition.
4. **Appendix E dates not individually verified.** Rows marked † flag known disagreements; unmarked rows haven't been checked against other sources.
5. **Part of the scanned James text was garbled** (toy timeline), so Appendix E uses commonly cited dates for toys.
6. **The Fake Food Cookbook PDF is an OCR scan.** A few quantities were unreadable: popcorn piece size (FF 51–52), doughnut-pan fill depth (FF 73), and the wonton dough's salt and water amounts (FF 65). Ch. 41 and its recipe cards describe these instead of giving numbers. FF's product names and prices (2018) are unverified.
7. **KF product names and convention rules are unverified.** One figure may be a typo: magnets "7 mm radius and 2 mm high" (KF 18).
8. **B's software, prices and filament data are mostly unverified.** Only Meshmixer, Fusion 360, Slic3r, Creative Commons and NIOSH were checked. B's rubric tables were reconstructed from the layout text; the project file gives criteria names and score levels only. One figure may be a typo: "lower the height to 0.5mm" to reveal detail (B 65). B spells the wax-printer maker "Soldiscape" (B 109); the guide doesn't name it.
9. **Restructure follow-ups.** Some chapters that were split (e.g. 11, 31, 34, 46) still have key-term lists or classroom prompts covering material that moved; tidy these on the next pass. Tutorial steps in the new projects files that aren't from a source are marked [General knowledge]; some sourced steps cite a section's page range rather than an exact page.

10. **Single-source chapters.** Chs. 33 (EVA, from KF), 40 (3D printing, from B) and 41 (prop food, from FF) still read largely as one book's method ("KF says…"). When a new book covers those materials, fold it in and rewrite toward a single synthesis, per CLAUDE.md Step 3.
