# Fabric and Upholstery Projects (projects for ch. 34)

Starter projects for [ch. 34](34-fabric-and-upholstery.md), following Hart's sequence for learning by finishing small objects: a pillowcase for sewing, then a recovered drop seat for upholstery (H 9). Steps marked **[General knowledge]** aren't from the guide's sources.

## Project 1: a pillowcase (H 9) [General knowledge]

**Teaches:** cutting, straight seams, hems, pressing.
1. Measure a pillow and add seam allowance (¼–⅝") and a hem allowance (H 201–212).
2. Mark on the **wrong side** and cut flat on the table with fabric-only scissors (H 201–212).
3. Sew the side and bottom seams **right sides together** (H 201–212).
4. Finish the raw edges with a serger, zigzag or a French seam (H 202–205, 210–212).
5. Turn and press a double-turned hem at the opening (H 210–212).
6. Turn right side out, push the corners out, and press.

## Project 2: a boxed cushion with piping (H 202–217) [General knowledge]

**Teaches:** boxing strips, piping, zips or slip-stitch closures.
1. Cut a foam insert ([Foam Projects](32-foams-and-rubbers-projects.md)) and wrap it in batting.
2. Cut a top, a bottom and a boxing strip, plus bias strips for piping.
3. Make **piping**: wrap cord in the bias strip and sew close with a piping foot (H 215–217).
4. Sew piping around the top and bottom panels.
5. Sew the boxing strip between them, clipping and notching at the corners (H 202–205).
6. Leave one side open; insert the cushion and close with a **slip (ladder) stitch** (H 206–210).

## Project 3: a recovered drop seat (H 9, 212–217)

**Teaches:** the upholstery sandwich and stapling order.
1. Remove the seat; strip old fabric if needed.
2. Build the sandwich: **plywood base → polyurethane foam traced from the base → batting → optional muslin → upholstery fabric** (H 212–217).
3. Mark the centre of each side on the base and the fabric.
4. Staple **centre front, centre back, centre right, centre left**, then work out toward the corners with even tension (H 212–217).
5. **Fold, trim and miter** each corner, keeping pleats close together (H 212–217).
6. Staple a **cambric** dust cover underneath (H 212–217).
7. Refit the seat.

## Project 4: a tufted cushion or panel (H 215–217) [General knowledge]

1. Mark a diamond grid on the plywood base and drill a hole at each point.
2. Build the foam and batting sandwich; cut holes in the foam at each point.
3. Lay the fabric over, leaving extra for the folds.
4. Pull covered buttons through to the back with twine and a long upholstery needle, and staple the twine.
5. Fold the fabric into neat pleats between buttons, then staple the edges.

## Quick reference

**Seams** (H 202–205): plain; flat-felled (strong, finished both sides); French (encloses raw edges, for sheers).

**Hand stitches** (H 206–210): running/basting; back stitch (strongest); whip/overcast; catch stitch (invisible hems); slip/ladder stitch (invisible closure).

**Machine tension** (H 206–207): too tight puckers or breaks the thread; too loose makes a bird's nest underneath. Test on a scrap of the same fabric.
