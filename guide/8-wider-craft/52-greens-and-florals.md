# 52. Greens and Florals

> **Sources:** M ch.1 (pp. 13–14). Most of this chapter is **[General knowledge]**: none of the guide's sources covers plants and flowers in depth.
> **[Check current]** Flame-retardancy rules for plants and fabric vary by venue and jurisdiction. Your fire marshal decides what's allowed.

Plants and flowers appear in almost every production: bouquets, potted plants, wreaths, gardens, Christmas trees, a single rose. Mussman lists foliage as part of a basic prop stock (M 13–14). In film and TV, a dedicated **greens** department often handles living and artificial plants on set.

## Real or artificial? [General knowledge]

| | Real (live or cut) | Artificial (silk, plastic, latex) | Dried or preserved |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Look** | Most convincing up close | Very good from a distance; best ones convincing close up | Period and autumnal looks |
| **Life** | Cut flowers last days; live plants need light and water | Years, with cleaning | Long, but fragile |
| **Fire** | Fresh plants are less flammable; dried ones very flammable | Often flammable; must be treated | **Highly flammable**; must be treated |
| **Allergies** | Pollen and scent can affect performers | None | Dust |
| **Cost over a run** | Replacement for every show adds up | One purchase | One purchase |

For most stage uses, **artificial flowers** are the default: consistent, durable and reusable. Real flowers suit short runs, close-up film work and scenes where an actor must smell, eat or tear them.

## Working with artificial flowers [General knowledge]

- **Buy better stems for hero props** (the bouquet an actor carries or the rose they hand over) and cheaper ones for distant dressing.
- **Shape them**: most stems have wire inside. Bend petals and leaves into natural positions; flat, identical stems look fake.
- **Age and colour them**: tint with thin paint or dye; add browning or wilting for age; dust and matte down shiny plastic ([ch. 28](../4-fabrication-core/28-painting-and-finishing.md)).
- **Arranging**: use floral foam, chicken wire or a pin frog in the container. Secure the arrangement so it doesn't tip when moved; weight light containers.
- **Clean** with compressed air or a soft brush between productions, and store in boxes away from crushing and sunlight.

## Building greenery [General knowledge]

- **Garlands and hedges**: artificial garland wired to a frame, chicken wire or netting ([ch. 26](../4-fabrication-core/26-structure-and-armatures.md)).
- **Trees**: real branches or a steel or wood armature, wrapped and textured for bark ([ch. 27](../4-fabrication-core/27-surfaces-and-texture.md)), with artificial foliage wired on.
- **Breakaway or tearable flowers**: tissue or crepe-paper flowers for petals pulled off nightly; buy or make enough for the run.
- **Food plants and produce** are covered with fake food ([ch. 41](../5-materials/41-prop-food.md)).

## Live plants onstage [General knowledge]

- Give them **light and water** between shows, or rotate several plants.
- Check for **pests and soil** before bringing plants into the building.
- Protect the stage floor from water with saucers and liners.
- Check with performers for allergies, especially to strongly scented flowers.

## Fire safety [General knowledge]

- **Dried, artificial and paper plants near lights or flame** must be treated with flame retardant or bought pre-treated, and tested as your fire marshal requires ([ch. 27](../4-fabrication-core/27-surfaces-and-texture.md#flame-retardancy-h-320)).
- Keep plants away from hot lamps and live flame ([ch. 48](../7-effects/48-flame-smoke-and-sound.md)).
- Real Christmas trees dry out fast and can be a serious fire risk; many venues ban them.

## For the classroom

- **Hero bouquet.** Students make a bridal or funeral bouquet from artificial stems, shaped and tinted to read as real at a given distance, then compare it with a real one.
- **Fire test.** Under supervision and with your fire marshal's procedure, test treated and untreated samples of artificial, dried and paper foliage.
- **Garden scene budget.** Students plan the greens for a garden scene three ways (all real, all artificial, mixed) and compare cost over a four-week run.
