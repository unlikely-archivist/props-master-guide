## Copyright note (for the textbook)

This guide **summarizes and reorganizes** ideas from the sources in original wording, with citations. It reproduces no images or extended passages. If you develop it into a textbook:
- Keep writing in **your own words and structure**, and cite the sources for facts, methods and examples you draw from them.
- **Brief quotations** with attribution are generally acceptable; long passages, figures, tables reproduced wholesale, and the authors' sample forms need **publisher permission**. (Mussman's book grants purchasers permission to adapt its sample forms; check the terms on its copyright page.)
- The templates in [Appendix B](B-paperwork-templates.md) are original designs and can be adapted freely.
- Get your publisher's or institution's guidance on fair use before publication.
