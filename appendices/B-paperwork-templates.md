## 5. Rental/Loan Agreement Checklist

*A checklist of what an agreement should cover (Mussman chs. 4, 6, 8). **Have your institution's legal or business office approve the actual form.** Use it in both directions, when you borrow and when you lend.*

- ☐ Type: **Loan** (free) or **Rental** (fee)
- ☐ Lender/owner: name, organization, contact details
- ☐ Borrower: name, title, organization, contact details
- ☐ Production, venue and run dates
- ☐ **Itemized list**: description, quantity, **condition at checkout** (photos attached), replacement value
- ☐ Pickup date and **return date**; who transports
- ☐ Fee, deposit and payment terms
- ☐ **Alterations**: none without written permission (list any agreed ones, e.g., "may be secured to deck with screws through underside of rail")
- ☐ Care and handling requirements (cleaning, climate, no food or drink, etc.)
- ☐ **Loss or damage**: who pays and how much (e.g., repair cost or full value; deposit forfeited)
- ☐ Late-return terms
- ☐ Insurance coverage (whose policy)
- ☐ Credit line in the program? (Confirm with marketing before the print deadline)
- ☐ Signatures and dates from both parties; **each keeps a copy**
- ☐ After return: condition checked and signed off; thank-you note sent

Give stage management a **list of every borrowed and rented item** for the run (ch. 11).

## 10. Strike & Returns Checklist (ch. 13)

**Before strike**
- ☐ Strike plan coordinated with the TD and ME (who starts when; staging area; vehicle schedule)
- ☐ Return appointments booked with every lender (**at least a week ahead**)
- ☐ Strike crew, tools, boxes, crates and baskets arranged

**Strike**
- ☐ Safety briefing (same weights and hazards as load-in)
- ☐ Borrowed and rented items packed separately, labeled by owner
- ☐ Distressing removed; items cleaned and repaired to original condition

**After**
- ☐ Each lender's items returned **in one trip**; condition signed off
- ☐ Thank-you notes sent
- ☐ Stock items cleaned and shelved in the correct sections; inventory updated with photos
- ☐ Show-specific and damaged items recycled or discarded
- ☐ Budget closed: receipts, POs and reimbursements submitted
- ☐ Prop bible archived; build notes and **time journal** filed for future estimates

## 13. "Actor's-Eye" Handoff Inspection

*Do this before any prop goes to rehearsal or the stage (M 125; H 41, 121, 157).*

- ☐ Hands run over every surface (carefully on metal); no splinters, burrs, staples or screw tips
- ☐ Edges broken; tube ends deburred; blades dulled
- ☐ Survives the **worst-case** use (stood on → jumped on; dropped → thrown)
- ☐ Weight and balance suit the actor; handholds where needed
- ☐ Finish doesn't rub off on hands or costumes (sealed)
- ☐ No uncured coatings or lingering fumes (stain, resin, solvent)
- ☐ Fits its path: costume pocket, doorway, prop-table space
- ☐ Flame-retardancy requirement met
- ☐ Care and operating notes given to stage management
