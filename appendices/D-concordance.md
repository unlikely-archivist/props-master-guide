# Appendix D: Concordance (Topic → Source Pages)

Use this to trace any topic back to the source books. **M** = Mussman (2008), book pages. **H** = Hart, *Prop Building Guidebook*, **1st ed. (2013)** pages. Later editions are paginated differently, but Hart's chapter titles remain a good guide. **HE** = Hart, *Prop Effects Guidebook*, chapter numbers from the 1st-edition table of contents (text not yet incorporated). Chapter numbers in the right-hand columns of the subject tables are **guide** chapters. **J** = James, *The What, Where, When of Theater Props* (1992), book pages.
